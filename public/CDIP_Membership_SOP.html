Program Overview
Virginia Institute of Finance and Management (VIFM) · Internal Use Only · Effective July 1, 2026
This SOP governs the full lifecycle of the CDIP annual membership subscription, from the 2026 Founding Member launch through ongoing annual renewal cycles. It covers renewal operations, benefits fulfillment, CPE tracking, suspension, and reinstatement. All staff executing any part of the CDIP program must be familiar with this document in its entirety.

Section 6 of 9 — Phase 5
Grace Period
July 2 – October 1, 2026 · Goal: Give non-renewed members a final 3-month window to reinstate with a $30 late fee, then suspend all who remain non-renewed on October 1.
A $30 late fee applies to all renewals processed during the grace period. Total cost becomes $80 (Founding Member) or $100 (Standard). No exceptions without VIFM leadership approval.
Grace Period Outreach
Grace Period Reminder #1: Send to all non-renewed members. State that the $30 late fee is now in effect and benefits remain suspended until renewal is completed.
VIFMJul 15
Grace Period Reminder #2: Escalate urgency. Emphasize that access to the CDIP Registry, e-Learning Portal, and DIBoK is suspended. Include a direct renewal link.
VIFMAug 15
Final Grace Period Warning: Last chance communication. Suspension is effective October 1. After suspension, reinstatement requires a re-application process if more than 12 months pass.
VIFMSep 15
Suspension Processing — October 1
Prepare the final suspension list: all members who remain non-renewed as of September 30. Share with Operations for processing.
VIFMSep 30
Process suspensions: remove all non-renewed members from the CDIP public Registry
OperationsOct 1
Revoke e-Learning Portal access for all suspended members
OperationsOct 1
Send suspension notification email to all affected members. Include: what has changed, the 12-month reinstatement window, reinstatement fee (dues + $30), and steps to reinstate.

Section 8 of 9 — Annual Cycle
Annual Cycle (Year 2+)
Repeating cycle beginning May each year · Standard renewal rate: $70 USD for all members. The Founding Member discounted rate of $50 USD applied to Year 1 (2026) only.
From Year 2 onwards, all members — including Founding Members — renew at the standard annual rate of $70 USD. The Founding Member designation and benefits (badge, registry recognition) remain active as long as membership is maintained. Repeat Phases 3, 4, and 5 each year.
Pre-Campaign Preparation (May Each Year)
Review and confirm fees, CPE requirements, and any policy changes for the upcoming renewal year. Obtain VIFM leadership sign-off before communicating to members.
VIFMMay annually

Section 9 of 9 — CPE Administration
CPE Administration
Ongoing · Standard procedures for receiving, verifying, approving, and auditing continuing professional education submissions from all active CDIP members.
All active CDIP members must complete 20 CPE hours per renewal year (July 1 – June 30). Members are responsible for submitting their hours and retaining documentation for a minimum of 2 years. VIFM reserves the right to audit any member's CPE records at any time.

CPE Submission Processing Procedure
Step 1 — Receive CPE submission via the online form or email. Confirm all required fields are present: activity type, hours claimed, activity date, provider name, and supporting documentation.
VIFM
Step 2 — Verify eligibility: confirm the activity qualifies under the CPE Policy, check that the annual maximum for that activity type has not been exceeded, and confirm the activity falls within the current CPE year.
VIFM
Step 3a — If approved: update the member's CPE total in the system and send an approval confirmation email with the new running total and remaining hours needed.
Operations
Step 3b — If rejected: send a rejection email with a clear reason and specific guidance on how to resubmit. Do not update the CPE total.
Operations
Documentation and Audit Procedure
Communicate the documentation retention requirement to all members annually: all CPE records must be retained for a minimum of 2 years (certificates, attendance records, self-study logs).
VIFM
Select annual audit sample (random). Issue written audit notice to selected members requiring documentation submission within 30 days. Log audit initiation date.
VIFMAnnually
Review all submitted audit documentation against logged CPE totals. Approve compliant submissions; escalate non-compliant or missing documentation cases to VIFM leadership for a determination within 14 days.
VIFMWithin 30 days of audit request
